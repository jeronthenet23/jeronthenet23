Well, here it is. My little piece of the internet, self-hosted on a Dell PowerEdge R720 in Georgetown, CA — served through a Cloudflare tunnel over Starlink because CGNAT wasn't going to stop me.

## Why I built this

I've been running a homelab for a while now. Emby, Sonarr, Radarr, SABnzbd, Homebridge — the whole stack. At some point you start wanting a place to document what you've built, share some photos, and just have a space that's yours.

So here we are.

## What to expect

I'll write about whatever I'm working on:

- **Homelab stuff** — server builds, Docker configs, networking, self-hosting
- **Photography** — I shoot landscapes and whatever catches my eye around the Sierra Nevada foothills
- **Ham radio** — I'm K6JRN, licensed and on the air
- **3D printing** — running a Bambu Lab H2S, mostly functional prints
- **Fire department** — volunteer with the local department

No schedule. No algorithm. Just posts when I have something worth sharing.

## The stack

This site runs on a Flask server inside Docker, fronted by Cloudflare Tunnel. Blog posts are markdown files in a GitHub repo, fetched and rendered client-side. Photos live in a local folder on the server.

Simple. Owned. Mine.
